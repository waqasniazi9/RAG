📚 Capstone-08: Company Knowledge Assistant

I built a RAG-based chatbot interface with Streamlit, LangChain, OpenAI and FAISS.

The pipeline loads fixed local TXT and Excel files, splits their content into chunks, creates embeddings, retrieves the top three relevant passages and generates a context-grounded answer.

Features:
• Cached startup indexing
• Policy and product-data questions
• Supporting source passages
• Chat history
• An “I don't know” fallback for unsupported questions

I tested it using fictional demo company data. [Use this statement only after completing the manual tests.]

An important learning: RAG guardrails reduce unsupported answers but do not guarantee factuality. Document privacy, retrieval quality and no-answer testing matter.
